About

I’m Megan, a learning designer, STEM educator, and project manager who loves working with experts to turn complex ideas into meaningful learning experiences. I’m especially interested in helping people see how what they’re learning connects to their lives, their work, and the questions they’re curious about.

My background is in physics and science education, and my work has taken me from hands-on STEM classrooms to online courses for learners around the world. At Duke, I partner with faculty and other experts across disciplines to design experiences that give learners opportunities to explore, practice, and build confidence.

I start by listening to the people sharing their expertise and the people we hope to reach. From there, I help shape the learning experience and build activities, tools, and resources that support it. Expanding access to education is what brings this work together for me. I want learning to feel approachable, relevant, and full of possibility.

Thanks for stopping by!

If you’re thinking through a learning challenge, interested in working together, or just want to say hello, I’d love to hear from you.
