02 · Background

How I got here

My work started in physics and grew into teaching, curriculum, learning design, and project management. Over time, those threads have blended into one practice.

2009 · B.S. in Physics, UNC Greensboro

Undergraduate research on stellar spectra and mathematical biology.

2011 · M.S. in Engineering Physics, Appalachian State University

Focused on instrumentation and automation, and started designing and teaching hands-on STEM courses for Duke TIP.

2013 · UNC Greensboro

Took on my first instructional design and project management role and launched the university’s first MOOC.

2014 · M.Ed. in Science Education, NC State University

Studied STEM education research and curriculum development, then taught middle and high school science.

2020 · Duke University

Joined Duke as a learning experience designer as teaching moved online.

Today · Senior Learning Experience Designer and PMP®

Lead learning design and project delivery, and stay close to teaching, most recently as a flight director for virtual Moon and Mars missions.

Next · Career interests

Learning products that give people more ways to explore, practice, and connect what they’re learning to their lives, with AI used thoughtfully.

03 · Teaching and curriculum

Where I’ve taught

I’ve taught a range of STEM subjects and learners at different levels. Many of those experiences called for me to develop the curriculum and the learning design together. I see them as closely connected: the curriculum gives us a meaningful destination, and the learning design helps learners find their way there.
