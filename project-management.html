01 · The lifecycle

Five phases for every project

The approach changes what happens inside each phase, but the phases stay the same.
01ScopeDefine goals, audience, deliverables, and constraints with partners.
02KickoffAlign the team on roles, schedule, and how we’ll work together.
03ManageKeep development on track while the work takes shape.
04DeliverLaunch, hand off, and capture what we learned.
05EvaluateReview how the product is working and plan what comes next.
02 · Choosing an approach

Aligned to project needs

I choose during scoping, based on how defined the work is and how often partners can review it.
AgileBest when
The product will change as we learn
Partners can give frequent feedback
Working versions matter more than a full spec
Example: AI Pilot
HybridBest when
The plan is set but details will evolve
Partners review work in batches
Some pieces need room to iterate
Example: Ethics, Equity, and Patient Safety in AI for Nursing Practice
TraditionalBest when
Requirements are clear from the start
Deadlines and approvals are fixed
Each phase builds on the one before
Example: Data Science with R

04 · Hybrid

Hybrid

A fixed roadmap of phases and sign-offs, with scheduled cycles of review, feedback, and implementation inside it.
1Kickoff & discovery
2Design & alignment
3Co-design
4Production & build
5Review & launch
↻ Review, feedback, implement

✓Review rounds are built in.

05 · Traditional

Traditional (Waterfall)

Work moves through defined phases in order, with each phase reviewed and approved before the next begins.
1Initiate
2Plan
3Develop
4Review & QA
5Launch & close
