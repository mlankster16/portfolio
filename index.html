I’m a senior learning experience designer and PMP®-certified project manager. I work with experts to turn complex ideas into learning people can use. Along the way, I help teams decide what to focus on, how learners will practice, and what we can build well with the time and resources available.

Featured samples

These projects show different parts of my work: designing learning experiences, helping experts shape their ideas into online courses, and building tools for fellow designers. Together, they offer a look at how I make decisions, work with others, and find practical ways to support learning.

Space Medicine A Duke course on Coursera that I managed and designed, with nearly 30,000 learners since 2021. I reimagined one week with an interactive exercise after every video, following an 1862 balloon flight from first symptoms to a redesigned flight. View concept → Choose Your Own Adventure A concept I designed for a required undergraduate science course. An interactive walkthrough follows one learner through a unit, showing how their interests and prior knowledge could shape the activities and support while the learning goals stay the same. View concept → AI for Nurses A three-course series developed with Duke School of Nursing and Johnson & Johnson. I led learning design and project management, drawing on nurses’ experiences to connect AI concepts to everyday clinical practice. View project → Let’s Talk about Digital You A for-credit, interdisciplinary Duke course in Canvas, open to students in any major. As project manager and lead learning experience designer, I helped two lead instructors and weekly guest experts build one integrated course on the technical and ethical sides of a data-centric world.
