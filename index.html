Megan Lancaster

I’m a Senior Learning Experience Designer, STEM Educator, and PMP®-certified Project Manager. I work with experts to turn complex science into clear curriculum, hands-on practice, and technology-enabled learning people can use. Along the way, I help teams decide what to focus on, how learners will practice, and what we can build well with the time and resources available.
