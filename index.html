Megan Lancaster

From K–12 classrooms to Coursera, I make complex STEM concepts clear, hands-on, and fun, and I love coaching others to design their own learning.

I design learning experiences and manage the projects that bring them to life. Lately, some of my favorite work happens with AI, tinkering with different tools and models to see what each can do, and every experiment sends me back to my designs with new questions.

Who I design withI start with the people I’m designing for and work closely with faculty and subject experts. Together, we figure out what learners need and how we can help.
How I designI choose methods that fit the work, from action maps for job training to knowledge graphs for connected ideas. Early prototypes give us something to try and discuss.My learning design approach →
How I deliverAs a PMP®-certified Project Manager, I help teams plan the work, make decisions, and stay connected from kickoff to launch, with room to adjust as we learn.My project management approach →
