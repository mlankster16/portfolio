Megan Lancaster

Senior Learning Experience DesignerSTEM EducatorPMP®

From K–12 classrooms to Coursera, I make complex STEM concepts clear, hands-on, and fun, and I love coaching others to design their own learning.

I design learning experiences and manage the projects that bring them to life. I start with the people I’m designing for, work closely with faculty and experts, and choose the method that fits: an action map for job training, a knowledge graph for connected ideas, a prototype when it’s easier to show than describe. As a PMP®, I keep teams, timelines, and partners moving from kickoff to launch. You can read more about my learning design and project management approaches.
