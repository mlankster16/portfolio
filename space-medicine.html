What works well on Coursera

For this course, accessibility and a clear modular structure mattered more than interactivity or immersion.

  • AccessibleVideos with captions and transcripts, readings, and quizzes work across devices and with assistive technology.
  • ModularEvery week follows the same pattern, so learners always know where they are and what comes next.
  • Self-pacedShort videos fit into a busy day, and learners can move through each week on their own schedule.
