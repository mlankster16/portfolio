How to explore this module

Each video has a short exercise after it. You can start at the top or jump to whatever looks interesting.

  1. Pick any item in the left menu. Gold numbers mark the five exercises.
  2. Videos are summarized. The instructors’ videos aren’t included, so each slot shows the title card and lists what the video covers.
  3. Turn on Design notes in the top bar to see the objective, rationale, and timing for each item.
  4. Your progress saves in this browser. Reopen this guide anytime with the ? button.
