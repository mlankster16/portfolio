Worked example Picture one breath of air as a pie chart. The orange slice is oxygen, about 21% of the air. The blue is everything else, mostly nitrogen. As you climb, the slice stays 21%, but the whole circle gets smaller because there’s less air in each breath. So oxygen per breath = 21 × pressure. Sea level: 21 × 1.0 = 21. Denver, at 80% pressure: 21 × 0.8 = about 17.
