The Score and the Bedside

Designer’s notes

Misconception this activity addresses“The score sees the whole patient.”

Why I built it this way

In our co-design session, nurses described things they notice at the bedside that never make it into the data behind a risk score. Here students sort each thing they notice, so they find the gap between the chart and the room on their own instead of reading about it.

Where it comes from

Based on a Course 2 example that shows what the model used next to what the nurse knows. Course 3 cases ask nurses to sort things the same way before they act.

The activity is written for students, the nurses taking these courses. These notes are for you.
