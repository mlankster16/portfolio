Cleared for Flight

A curriculum concept for training field employees to fly drone inspections, built up from the tasks of the job.
Start with the task analysis ↓Try the scenario ↓
LearnersNew field employees with no prior flight experience
GoalQualified to plan and fly an asset inspection on their own
DeliverySelf-paced, classroom, simulator and field
StatusConcept sample. The organization, aircraft and procedures are generic.
01 · Task analysis

What the job requires

I start by breaking the job into duties, tasks and steps, then rate each task by how much harm a mistake could cause. Every task becomes a performance objective with a condition, a behavior and a standard. Select a duty and a task to see how it breaks down.

02 · Course map

From self-paced to the field

Each lesson uses the modality that suits its tasks. Knowledge is self-paced, judgment is practiced in the simulator, and anything that touches the real aircraft happens with an instructor. Filter by modality to see where each one carries the load.

03 · Simulator scenario

Decisions under pressure

Scenario scripts put the critical tasks into situations where the right call isn’t obvious. Each decision point is tied to an evaluation criterion, so the simulator records what the trainee chose and the instructor can debrief from it. Try the substation inspection.

04 · Practical evaluation

The field checkride

Qualification depends on what the trainee does with a real aircraft, scored by an instructor against the same standards as the objectives. Missing any critical item means the trainee isn’t qualified yet, however well the rest went. Mark items as met to see the result.

05 · Measuring effectiveness

What changes on the job

Measures are planned with the curriculum, so the data needed to revise it is collected from the first cohort.
During trainingSimulator decisions and attempts per scenario, sent to the LMS as xAPI statements
At qualificationFirst-attempt checkride pass rate and the items missed most often
On the jobIncident and near-miss reports, and how often inspection data has to be re-flown
When an item is missed often, I trace it back through the course map to the lesson and objective behind it, and revise there first.
